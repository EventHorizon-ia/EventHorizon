Evidence & limitations

Reported validation result, negative economic outcome

⚠️

Promising validation signal; tradability not established

The supplied dashboard reports 60.5% accuracy at 5 seconds against its 52% reference (+8.5 percentage points). Public research summaries report 60.7% for an H0-focused model and 59.9% for H0 in a multi-horizon model. They also state that the validation set was used for checkpoint selection, not as a final independent test. The exact 60.5% run and its p-value / confidence interval remain unverified from the public summary.

The project README reports that the strategy was economically unviable under the tested maker-execution assumptions, and that maker-fee sensitivity was evaluated separately. The public summary does not establish the old page’s more specific claim that net PnL was negative across every Binance VIP fee tier; that tier-by-tier statement should be checked against the underlying result files before being restored.

These findings are historical and specific to the evaluated data, checkpoint and execution assumptions. Validation does not guarantee future performance, and a positive classification metric is not evidence of profitability.
